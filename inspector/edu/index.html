The learning path

From “what happened?”
to “here’s the evidence.”

Two ways into the same habit: observe carefully, try a bounded action, then explain what you found.

01 / FOLLOW THE STORY

Story Mode

Join Mira’s Coffee Shop Recon. Follow seven fictional story beats through sample café traffic, a simulated disconnect and rejoin, a welcome-page Rickroll and a fictional FBI finale.

Learn through context, cause and consequence.

02 / TRY THE TASK

Task Mode

Open Task 01: Information Gathering. Work through three displayed sample commands to identify a local user, label a host and inspect fictional interfaces.

Turn a guided step into evidence you can describe.

03 / EXPLAIN YOUR FINDINGS

Read the record

Watch the evidence record below the preview update as you progress. Each entry is a fixed sample that gives a learner and instructor something concrete to discuss.
