BStudioB product / Early concept

Earquity

Exploring a more transparent way to discover and support emerging music creators.

An idea in development

Know where your listening goes.

Earquity is a proposed pay-as-you-go music platform where artists set a price for their music and listeners can see the music allocation and platform fee separately.

The concept is intended to support smaller human creators, with guidance to help artists promote their work. Pricing, eligibility, rights and payment arrangements are still being developed.

Earquity is an early concept; the app is not yet available. Its development repository is private and accessible to authorised collaborators.
