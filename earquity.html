THE SONG AND THE RECORDING

Where does
PRS fit in?

Songwriting and recording are different rights. PRS for Music represents performing rights in works, and MCPS represents mechanical rights for its repertoire. An appropriate licence and accurate usage reports may be required for on-demand listening.

Where a collecting society handles rights, it distributes royalties under its rules and the registered interests. A recording rights holder may pay musicians, producers or engineers according to their agreements; their role alone does not guarantee an ongoing share.

Earquity’s aim is to show what is known: an agreed allocation, who receives it, the payment route and whether payment is verified. The example above does not establish a per-play PRS payment or a confirmed integration.
