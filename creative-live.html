One service plan, room-wide

Church

What it offers

Order songs and service moments, browse the included KJV by book and chapter, choose a verse range, and present the same live state to each receiver.

How it works

Build the running order on the host. Select a song or passage, then navigate from Controller or Performer while Audience screens stay synchronized over the local network.

Compared with a split setup

Instead of switching between separate lyric, scripture and display tools, keep those cues in one running order. Keep a spare manual backup for live services.

Meeting content without the scramble

Work

What it offers

Keep an agenda, locally imported PDF or PowerPoint slides, supported media and presenter/audience screens in one operator workflow.

How it works

Import the deck on the host, order presentation items, and open an Audience screen for the room. Use Performer as an optional confidence monitor; OBS remains the camera and streaming tool.

Compared with a split setup

Reduce app and window switching between a slide deck and separate display controls. Tributary does not replace PowerPoint authoring, video conferencing or OBS production.

A live-performance workspace

Artist

What it offers

Chord-aware songbooks, set arrangements, musician-specific monitors, performer notes, show timing, backing-track transport and mapped pedal/MIDI/OSC controls.

How it works

Build and rehearse a set, assign performer roles, and navigate sections or tracks from Controller, Performer or mapped hardware. Audience output remains independently configurable.

Compared with a split setup

Bring charts, lyrics, cues and local backing playback into one session instead of juggling separate paper charts and lyric screens. Professional multichannel routing remains a separate audio workflow.
