Why BStudioB exists · independent UK studio

Useful technology should fit people.

BStudioB is an independent software studio created by Nathan Brown-Bennett. We build focused digital products and help organisations improve the systems and workflows they already rely on.

Our starting point is simple: understand what people need to do, then make the tools around that work more useful.

Invest in us ↗

Interested in backing the studio? Start a conversation through our enquiry form; funding details are discussed directly.

Why it matters

Disconnected tools, repetitive admin and awkward handoffs can make meaningful work harder than it needs to be. We see a need for technology and workflows that respond to real people and real contexts.

Our approach

Understand the need first. Improve existing systems where that is the right fit. Build new software when it serves a clear purpose, with honest boundaries around what it can do.

Our creator

Nathan Brown-Bennett created BStudioB to bring product ideas, software and practical problem-solving together in one independent studio.

Our aims

Build a considered portfolio of products and scoped services that help people work, learn, create and connect with greater clarity.
