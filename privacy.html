Privacy notice

Privacy.

What this site collects

The public site does not use advertising cookies, account registration or payment processing. We use Umami Cloud for anonymous, cookieless website statistics such as page views, referrers, device type and broad location. We do not send names, email addresses or form contents to analytics. If you use a waitlist form, we collect the email address you enter, the product you selected and the time of your request. If you email us directly, we receive the information in that message.

Why we use it

We use waitlist information to manage your interest in the relevant product, respond to enquiries and share occasional relevant launch, pilot or playtest updates. We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Analytics and form processing

Umami Cloud processes the anonymous analytics described above. The BStudioB property is currently on Umami’s free Hobby plan and uses its US data region; we will review the region and provider arrangement before collecting material traffic.

Waitlist and enquiry forms are processed by FormSubmit and delivered to the relevant BStudioB mailbox. FormSubmit processes the form submission to provide this service. We keep waitlist details only while the relevant launch, pilot or playtest is active. We keep enquiry details only while needed to respond, assess or manage the conversation, unless a longer period is required for legal or accounting records. You can ask us to remove them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or remove your waitlist details at any time by emailing hello@bstudiob.co.uk from the address you used. Please do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, confidential security information or other sensitive information through a waitlist form or ordinary email.

Changes

We may update this notice when the website or our services change. The latest version will always appear on this page.
