Privacy notice

Privacy.

What this site collects

The public site does not use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking, analytics, account registration or payment processing. If you use a waitlist form, we collect the email address you enter, the product you selected and the time of your request. If you email us directly, we receive the information in that message.

Why we use it

We use waitlist information to manage your interest in the relevant product, respond to enquiries and share occasional relevant launch, pilot or playtest updates. We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Form processing and retention

Waitlist forms are processed by FormSubmit and delivered to the relevant BStudioB mailbox. FormSubmit processes the form submission to provide this service. We keep waitlist details only while the relevant launch, pilot or playtest is active, or until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or remove your waitlist details at any time by emailing hello@bstudiob.co.uk from the address you used. Please do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents, confidential security information or other sensitive information through a waitlist form or ordinary email.

Changes

We may update this notice when the website or our services change. The latest version will always appear on this page.
