01 / THE IDEA

Keep the feeling.
Build the place.

We’re exploring a way to use photos and video you already have to create a personal brick interpretation of a location. Choose the scene and details that matter to you, then follow digital instructions to make it your own.

A MEMORY IN THE MAKING

to a model.

01 / YOUR MOMENT

Bring the memory

Start with photographs or video from a meaningful location.

02 / YOUR SCENE

Make it yours

Choose the parts of the place to include and explore the size and detail of your scene.

03 / YOUR BUILD

Make it real

Receive digital instructions and a parts list to build at your own pace.

This is the intended experience. Photo-to-scene reconstruction and build instructions are still being tested; ordering is not yet available. Physical kits may follow later.

02 / EXPLORING THE POSSIBILITIES

Little pieces.
A bigger idea.

We’ve started by experimenting with a flat mosaic and an approximate 3D brick study of Oxford’s Radcliffe Camera. These are research models, not finished products.

We’re still working on how to preserve the character of a place, choose suitable pieces and make a design that can be built and supported in real life.

Mosaic based on Radcliffe Camera by JuliaC2006, CC BY 2.0; cropped and converted to grayscale. The 3D study is an approximate map-based model with projected photo shading. 03 / MEMORIES TRAVEL

Some places
are worth sharing.

We imagine a community where people can choose to share finished brick scenes, explore favourites by place, time of day and weather, and help others capture locations through contributed walk-around videos.

Contributors could earn discounts towards a future purchase. Sharing would be optional, and the details are still being developed.

04 / PROJECT AND LICENSING

Made to keep.
Clear to use.

MemGos is being explored as a digital-instructions-first service. Licensing, pricing and the terms for using a design will be explained before any purchase is offered.

The intended home for the customer app is memgos.bstudiob.co.uk. It is not yet available. Physical kits may follow later.
